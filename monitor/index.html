A reusable AI-assisted development workflow for Claude Code. Every piece of work is a topic — documented, planned, tested, and deployed through a state graph of nodes: skills, rules, templates and gates that scale with risk. This explorer renders every file, rule and transition of the plugin as one interactive map.
